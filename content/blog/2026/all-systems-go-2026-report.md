We had a great time at this year's edition of All Systems Go! in Berlin.

[All Systems Go!](https://all-systems-go.io), a conference focused on foundational user-space Linux technologies, providing a meeting place for discussions relevant to contributors to projects that operate in this space.

There were multiple presentations about building and deploying OS images, including one from us about introducing Moonforge to make creating, building, and maintaining Yocto-based images using modern tools.
